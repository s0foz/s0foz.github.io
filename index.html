The Hybrid Sensor Node

Pioneering the Next Generation

Optical SWIR Stack

Multi-wavelength InP LEDs emitting at 850nm, 1550nm, and 2200nm to isolate glucose structures from dominant water absorption.

RF Microwave Sweep

1.5 - 4.0 GHz split-ring resonator reading complex permittivity, continuously cross-correcting the dielectric environment.

Environmental Cancellation

Onboard 6-axis IMU, GSR sweat sensors, and an NTC thermistor instantly filter out motion, temperature, and hydration artifacts.

Screenless Design

No screens. No distractions. The device processes data in the background and transmits securely via BLE to your smartphone app.
