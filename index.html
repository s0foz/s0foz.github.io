Device Culture

Review / The fold, one year on

The Folding Phone Is Learning to Disappear

Foldables have spent five years proving they can open. The interesting work is now in proving they can close without asking for attention: quieter hinges, flatter creases, software that stops treating the fold as an event.

The useful measure of a repairable object is not whether it can be opened once, but whether someone will still have the reason and the tools to open it five years later.
