HOW TO PLAY

Common signal — 1 point. Always on the grid.
Rare signal — 3 points. Fades after a while.
Premium signal — 10 points, but its value decays and it disappears. Go get it.
Power cell — slow-time, shield, phase, magnet, double score, trim, extra life, blip, surge or defrag.
Decoy signal — avoid it. Costs length and your chain.
Moving packet — a hazard block on a rhythm. Time it.

CONTROLS

Arrows or WASD to steer. P or Esc pauses. M mutes. Space starts and confirms. Swipe works on touch screens.

SCORING

Chains multiply value if you keep eating inside the window. Travelling further to a signal pays more. Near misses pay a trickle of bonus points. Zone clears pay a bonus scaled by your stars.
